\section*{Conclusion}
%\textcolor{red}{Estelle today}\\
This paper began with the problem of congestion and the research question of investigating the impact of DRT on city congestion in Capetown. A brief review of literature on DRT and its modelling with MATsim and related issues gave background for the paper. We hypothesised that DRT would be a beneficial addition to transport systems in developing cities like Cape Town. In the context of the Cape Town transport system, simulations were used to show that introducing medium sized fleets of DRT vehicles at double the price of public transport improves congestion outcomes significantly. Various scenarios were explored to find the optimal fleet size and pricing within the modelling constraints. The improvements are seen to impact mode share and switching, and waiting and travelling time, while having \textcolor{red}{negligible} \st{negligent} effects on emissions if electric vehicles are not used.
